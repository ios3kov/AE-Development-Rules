## Verification and next work

The original audit retained isolated probes, a 116-file hashed inventory, local self-test output and exact baseline CI/release identity outside the standard checkout. Local self-test passed available Node/POSIX/macOS checks; two Windows-only checks were skipped locally. Exact-baseline public CI passed all three operating systems. Actual AI behavior and AE product runtime were NOT RUN.

The [repeat audit](DEEP_AUDIT_5_0_0_REPEAT.md) re-established D01–D04 and added D05–D07. Its [redacted probe evidence](evidence/deep-v5-repeat-probes.json) records actual observations, controls and limitations. The [third audit](DEEP_AUDIT_5_0_0_THIRD.md) preserved those entries and added D08, with [separate evidence](evidence/deep-v5-third-probes.json). The [fourth audit](DEEP_AUDIT_5_0_0_FOURTH.md) added D09–D10 and [matching evidence](evidence/deep-v5-fourth-probes.json). At the end of the fourth audit there were ten open findings and four optional proposals; these historical counts were not an exhaustive-error guarantee or a quality score.

The [fifth audit](DEEP_AUDIT_5_0_0_FIFTH.md) reviewed eighteen normative interactions and nine declared routing contexts. It confirmed no new independent finding in that scope; [separate evidence](evidence/deep-v5-fifth-review.json) distinguishes manual text review from executed helper checks. At the end of that fifth audit counts remained ten OPEN and four PROPOSED. That audit did not close prior findings or certify actual AI/AE behavior.
